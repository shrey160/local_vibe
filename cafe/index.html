The Art of Pour-Over Coffee

1

Select Your Beans

Choose between our medium and dark roast beans, each carefully selected for their unique flavor profiles.

2

Weigh Your Grind

Precise measurements are crucial. We grind fresh, just before brewing to ensure maximum freshness.

3

Heat Your Water

Water between 195-205°F is ideal. Hot enough to extract flavors, but not so hot as to burn the coffee.

4

Bloom the Coffee

Pour just enough water to saturate the grounds and let them bloom for 30-45 seconds. This releases CO2.

5

Slow Pouring

Pour slowly in circular motions to evenly saturate the grounds, maintaining a steady flow rate.

6

Enjoy Your Brew

Once all water has passed through, you have a perfectly brewed cup of pour-over coffee.
