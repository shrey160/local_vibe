About Our Roast

At Coffee House, we blend traditional Ethiopian coffee with modern brewing methods to create unparalleled dark roasts. Sourced from the high altitudes of Ethiopia and Guatemala, our beans are fermented naturally and roasted at 640°F to bring out bold, chocolatey, and citrusy notes.

Our dark roast coffee is perfect for those who love rich flavors with hints of caramelized sweetness. Whether it's a morning cup or a late-night sipping session, our beans elevate every moment in life.

The Art of Brewing

#1

Coffee Selection

We curate premium coffee beans from the finest Arabica and Robusta varieties, each representing centuries of tradition and natural selection.

#2

Natural Fermentation

The beans undergo natural fermentation for 12 hours, developing complex fruit flavors while retaining their rich aroma.

#3

Light Roasting

Roasted at exactly 640°F, we eliminate all green coffee notes and transform them into deep chocolate, caramelized, and nutty flavors.

#4

Packaging

We keep our beans fresh until they're ready. Our packaging prevents moisture damage, ensuring maximum flavor and aroma.
